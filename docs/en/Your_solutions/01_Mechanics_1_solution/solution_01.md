## 1. Projectile Motion

Given:
v = 100 m/s  
θ = 37°

---

### Horizontal and vertical motion

vx = v cos(θ)  
vy = v sin(θ)

Horizontal:
ax = 0 → constant velocity  

Vertical:
ay = -g  

---

### Time of flight

T = (2vy) / g

≈ (2 × 100 × sin37°) / 9.81  
≈ (2 × 100 × 0.6) / 9.81  
≈ 120 / 9.81 ≈ 12.2 s  

---

### Maximum height

H = vy² / (2g)

≈ (60²) / (2 × 9.81)  
≈ 3600 / 19.62 ≈ 183.5 m  

---

### Range

R = vx × T  

vx ≈ 100 × cos37° ≈ 100 × 0.8 = 80  

R ≈ 80 × 12.2 ≈ 976 m  

---

### Final answers

T ≈ 12.2 s  
H ≈ 183.5 m  
R ≈ 976 m
